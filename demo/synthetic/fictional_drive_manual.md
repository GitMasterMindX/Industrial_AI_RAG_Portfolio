# Fictional Drive FD-100 — synthetic benchmark manual

All identifiers and procedures are fictional. Not a manufacturer manual or operating instruction.

## ALM-101: Overtemperature
Enclosure ventilation is blocked or ambient temperature is too high. Diagnose ventilation and ambient temperature; stop safely before inspection.

## ALM-202: Communication loss
Controller to drive communication was interrupted. Diagnose network link, connector, addressing and controller diagnostics; never bypass interlocks.

## STO: Safe Torque Off
Safe Torque Off prevents torque-producing power. Never bypass interlocks or safety circuits. Follow approved isolation procedures.

## ALM-303: DC bus undervoltage
Supply voltage is below the drive threshold. Check supply measurements using approved procedures and qualified personnel.

## ALM-404: Motor overload
Motor thermal load exceeds configured limit. Review load history, duty cycle and cooling; do not increase protection limits without engineering review.

## ALM-505: Encoder signal fault
Encoder feedback is invalid or missing. Inspect encoder status, connector and signal diagnostics under safe conditions.

## ALM-606: Parameter mismatch
Saved configuration does not match commissioned motor data. Compare configuration against approved commissioning records.

## ALM-707: Cooling fan failure
Cooling fan speed feedback is missing. Inspect fan diagnostics and arrange safe maintenance.

## ALM-808: Emergency stop circuit
Emergency stop safety circuit indicates an active stop. Do not bypass or reset until site safety procedures authorize it.

## ALM-909: Fieldbus timeout
Fieldbus cyclic telegram timeout exceeded. Inspect controller connection and telegram update diagnostics.

## PAR-010: Acceleration ramp
Acceleration ramp parameter sets the requested speed change duration. Changes require an approved commissioning procedure.

## PAR-020: Motor nominal current
Motor nominal current parameter is configured from motor nameplate data. Incorrect settings may compromise protection.
